# Zaaheen memory

Zaaheen is the person's own memory, kept encrypted on their computer and shared by all of their AI apps. Its tools are `memory_read`, `memory_search`, `memory_write`, `memory_update` and `memory_delete`. Each tool's own description says how to use it; follow it.

## Check Zaaheen first

Before answering anything about the person, their preferences, their work or their plans, ask Zaaheen with `memory_read`, even when your own memory has nothing. Ask one thing per call, as a question, and wait for each answer before the next.

## Save what lasts

When the person tells you something that will still be true next week, such as a preference, a decision, or a fact about them or their work, save it with `memory_write` in the format the tool describes. Do not save passwords, keys, or anything they asked you not to keep.

## When Zaaheen does not answer

- If the Zaaheen tools are missing or cannot connect, the Zaaheen app needs to be installed and open at least once, and signed in. It is at https://zaaheen.com. On a Mac, Zaaheen must be in the main Applications folder.
- If Zaaheen answers that it is locked, or that a trial or subscription has ended, tell the person what it said, in its words.
